# Cyberpunk Drift

A neon, rain-soaked street drifting game for the browser (three.js). Drift Hunters style handling and chain scoring with a Forza-like presentation.

- **City**: a grid of real roads (avenues up to 30 m wide, side streets, crosswalks, lane paint), raised sidewalks, towers with lit windows and shopfronts, neon signs, street lamps and an open drift lot with pylons.
- **Detail** (`src/cityDetail.js`): stepped tower tops, parapets, rooftop units with fans, water tanks, lattice masts with blinking beacons, AC units under the windows, vending machines, bins and rubbish bags, granite curbs, bollards, traffic lights that cycle, wooden utility poles strung with sagging power lines, manhole covers with steam, tyre stacks and floodlight towers in the drift lot. Windows have frames, ledges, blinds, room depth and the odd flickering TV. Everything is instanced, and small props skip the reflection pass.
- **Cars** (`src/car.js`): panel seams, bonnet vents, gills, door handles, fuel filler, canards, tow hooks, fog lamps, number plates, neon door decals, a visible cabin with bucket seats, harnesses, steering wheel and roll cage, per-car rims (six-spoke, mesh, deep dish, five-spoke) with lug nuts, tread grooves and sidewall lettering, and per-car exhausts each with its own backfire. At build time the car is baked into one mesh per material.
- **Street life** (`src/props.js`, `src/cityProps.js`): 50+ models placed with a per-sidewalk occupancy check: bus shelters with backlit ads, bus stop poles, benches, ad lightboxes, street trees (some strung with lights), traffic mirrors, hydrants, parking meters, road signs, street name plates, bikes and bike racks, scooters, guard rails, electrical cabinets, post boxes, phone booths, three brands of vending machine, ticket machines, recycling bins, planters, crates, cardboard, dumpsters, gas meters, roller shutters with graffiti, striped awnings, noren doorways, paper lanterns, menu boards, yatai food stalls, neon arches, lantern strings, direction gantries, storm drains; up the walls balconies (some with laundry or plants), fire escapes, drainpipes, satellite dishes and projecting box signs; on the roofs billboards, cooling towers, gardens, a helipad, and a construction site with scaffolding, hoarding and a tower crane. Everything is static-batched into one mesh per material per city chunk.
- **Surfaces**: buildings are concrete panel, brick, mosaic tile, ribbed metal or glass curtain wall, each with its own windows; asphalt has wheel tracks, cracks, repair patches and oil stains; pavements have slabs and yellow tactile strips at crossings; street lamps light the ground; lane arrows on the avenues. Signs come from procedural atlases (`src/textures.js`) with dozens of designs and invented brands.
- **Set pieces** (`src/setPieces.js`): LED screens with scrolling tickers and looping ads, a hologram over the drift lot, an ad blimp circling the city, delivery drones, and traffic cones in the lot you can knock flying.
- **Sound** (`src/audio.js`): engine loops rendered from real firing patterns (straight six, boxer four, cross-plane V8, twin-turbo six), crossfaded by rpm and load; turbo whistle, flutter and blow-off; supercharger whine; tyre squeal, overrun crackle and a street reverb.
- **Rain**: GPU rain streaks, lightning, wet asphalt with planar reflections, standing puddles that mirror the city and ripple with raindrops.
- **Handling** (`src/physics.js`): a four-wheel model with longitudinal and lateral load transfer, a combined-slip tyre with load sensitivity, and an engine, clutch and locked diff driving the rear axle, so power-over, lift-off, handbrake and clutch kicks all come out of the physics. Assists sit on top, Drift Hunters style: auto counter-steer (the stick steers relative to where the car is travelling), angle hold (throttle and steering into the slide ask for more angle), a soft angle limit and drift speed hold. Off, Low, Medium and High.
- **Scoring** (`src/drift.js`): angle × speed builds a chain, the multiplier climbs to ×5 while you stay sideways, the chain banks after 2.2 s, and a wall hit loses it.
- **Clutch kick** (Shift, or X on a controller): hold to rev the engine free, release to dump the revs into the rear tyres and throw the car sideways from higher gears.
- **Garage** (`src/garage.js`, `src/garageUI.js`): banked chains pay credits (crashes pay nothing). Turbo and supercharger kits per car (the Ronin comes twin-charged), modelled in the physics with turbo lag and instant supercharger boost. Four cars with their own handling, body, wing and engine voice: Ronin RS (starter), Kaze 86, Oni V8 and Ryujin GT. Each has three levels of engine, tyres, weight and angle-kit upgrades, plus free paint, neon and rim colours. Progress saves in the browser.
- **Feel**: skid marks that fade on the wet road, keyboard throttle that feathers in, a chase camera that pulls in instead of entering buildings, a slight lean in corners and a fine buzz at high speed.
- **Controls**: keyboard, gamepad and on-screen touch buttons.

## Develop

```sh
npm install
npm test        # headless handling checks for every car, stock and fully upgraded, plus garage logic
npm run build   # dist/index.html + dist/game.js (dist/preview.html for local testing)
```
